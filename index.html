Questions
What should I wear?
We’d love for our guests to wear colourful formal attire to help make our day colourful and vibrant!
When do I need to RSVP?
Hearing from you on or before July 1st would be ideal so we can get an idea for numbers etc.
Please RSVP through this website (below) or get in touch with us directly if there are any issues etc.
Can I take photos during the wedding?
We will be having an ‘unplugged’ ceremony, so we kindly ask that phones and cameras be kept out of sight until after the ceremony is finished. You are welcome to take photos after, but we kindly ask you not to post any pictures of us on social media until we have posted ourselves.
